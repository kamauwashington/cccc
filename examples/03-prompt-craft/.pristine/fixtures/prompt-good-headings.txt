## Goal

Write a script that reports the ten largest files in a repository.

## Constraints

Node 20, no dependencies outside the standard library. Skip node_modules.

## Output format

One file, scripts/big-files.mjs, plus a two line usage note in the README.

## Success criteria

`node scripts/big-files.mjs .` prints 10 rows, largest first, in under 2
seconds on this repository.

## What to avoid

Do not shell out to `du` or `find`. Do not follow symlinks.
